01 / Evidence before conviction

OUT-OF-SAMPLE CHECK

The board prices each row with the estimator that won out of sample in its family; families with fewer than eight comparables use the snapshot-wide median. All multiples are clamped to [0.05, 2.50].

A fitted curve was tested and lost to the median in most families, so the board keeps the median there.

Per-family validation, and the tested curve

Tested size curve: multiple = clamp(a + b × log10(TVL in USD), 0.05, 2.50). Ordinary least squares on raw market-cap / TVL; each family fits separately at n ≥ 8. The curve test uses the family median for smaller or degenerate training samples, and the snapshot median for empty ones.

What the validation measures

Each token-bearing protocol is held out once. Both its curve and any fallback median are rebuilt without it. Error is |predicted multiple − actual multiple|, in multiple points. Predicted / actual = 1.0 is perfect; within 2× means a ratio from 0.5 to 2.0. The baseline repeats the same holdouts using only medians. These scores test market multiples, not future token launches or farming returns. Raw outliers remain in the fit and errors; predictions alone are clamped. Families of exactly eight train on seven and therefore validate their median fallback. Ties keep the median. Overall scores above describe the tested curve and median baseline, not a separate validation of the selection rule.

No protocols match. Clear a filter to broaden the board.

Scenario, not a promised reward. Implied value = TVL × selected multiple × momentum × maturity. Momentum = 1 + 0.75 × clamp(7-day change, −40, 40) / 100; missing change uses 0. Maturity = 0.70 + 0.30 × min(age, 1095) / 1095; missing age uses 365 days. A 0.5% airdrop pool is divided equally among 10,000 participants, then weighted by 25% launch-and-eligibility probability. Capital is at risk; principal losses, taxes and labor are excluded. Gas and fees are fixed scenarios, not live quotes. “Farm” requires net EV ≥ $5 and gross EV ≥ twice costs; “thin” has positive net EV below those margins; “skip” has net EV ≤ $0.

Gas assumptions per transaction

Two entry transactions + one per started week + withdrawal and claim. Gas is budgeted even if no claim arrives. Stay on an already funded chain; cross-chain funding costs are excluded. Capital tiers follow TVL: $50 below $50M, then $75 / $100 / $150 / $250 at $50M / $250M / $1B / $5B. Fees = 0.3% of capital; opportunity cost = capital × 5% × days / 365.
